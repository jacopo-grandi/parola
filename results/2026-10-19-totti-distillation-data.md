## Sources

English meeting speech from the **AMI** and **ICSI** transcript annotations (NXT XML, text
only, no audio), both CC BY 4.0 — verified in decision 0004 and again on the download pages
and the packages' own `LICENCE.txt` (fetched 2026-10-19 from the official Edinburgh mirrors).
Sentences are rebuilt from the `words`/`segments` files by `parola.totti.distill`
(unit-tested on hand-built NXT examples); the verbatim feel — fillers, truncations, restarts —
is kept on purpose: it is the domain the student will see.

## Teacher pass

`parola totti distill` (resumable: flushes per batch, re-runs skip finished ids) translates
the sources with MADLAD-400-3B-MT CT2 int8, beam 2, CPU — measured at ~4.5 sentences/s, so
the full pass is ~5 hours. Output: `data/totti-train/distilled-madlad.jsonl`. One observed
teacher trait worth keeping in mind for T4: MADLAD tends to drop fillers ("uh") in the
Italian, so the student will learn subtitle-style output, not verbatim-preserving
translation — acceptable for ToTTI, whose output layer is subtitles, never the transcript.
## Outcome (same day)

The full pass finished: 77,088 pairs in ~6.5 h wall time (shared CPU; paused once for a
latency measurement and resumed with nothing lost). Quality filters (`distill.clean`,
unit-tested; declared, no language detector — the copy and ratio filters cover it):

| | pairs |
| --- | --- |
| distilled | 77,088 |
| dropped: length ratio outside [0.4, 1.6] | 1,184 |

Training files: `data/totti-train/clean-{train,dev}.jsonl`. A seeded 50-pair sample is in
`data/totti-train/review-sample.md` for the human check before any training (plan T3).

## Human sample review (same day)

Jacopo reviewed the 50 pairs in a local quiz page: **5 rejected, 6 corrected, 39 approved**
(90% pass). What the rejections taught, fed back into the pipeline:

- The teacher leaks HTML entities — `&apos;` for every Italian apostrophe (5,675 times),
  `&quot;` around quotes. Those are good translations with an encoding artifact: `clean` now
  **unescapes** them (5,242 pairs kept that a drop filter would have wasted).
- The remaining rejections are garbled or truncated ASR sources that the teacher "repairs"
  by inventing fluent text, plus one wrong sense ("crash" of software rendered as a road
  accident). No automatic filter catches invention without a reference; the 90% sample pass
  is the measure that the rate is tolerable for training.
- One register note for later: in his corrections Jacopo kept the fillers ("um", "uh") that
  the teacher drops. The subtitle layer currently follows the teacher (subtitle-style); if
  verbatim-preserving translation is ever wanted, that is a training-data decision, not a
  model one.

Final training set, review applied (rejected removed, corrections as human targets):
**72,522 train / 3,199 dev**. The review flow (quiz on localhost, results as JSON) is the
template for future rounds.

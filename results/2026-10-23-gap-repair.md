# Gap repair — 2026-10-23

Code: `cd996cc` plus the gap-repair change committed together with this report. Generated by `parola eval`, extra sections written by hand. Values in %, with the 95% confidence interval in brackets (bootstrap over segments).

## Why

On a real call (`call-20261023-113020`, mic track, 9 min) Whisper's long-form decode jumped a window with ~26 s of clean Italian speech (180.05–206.38 s) and a second one right after (211.76–237.07 s). It happens with and without VAD, so neither the VAD nor the downstream filters are at fault; each slice extracted on its own transcribes perfectly. Decision [0050](../docs/decisions/0050-gap-repair-for-skipped-windows.md): after the full decode, sound regions ≥ 3 s with no transcribed word on them are decoded again, each on its own clip (1.5 s margins, at most 8 clips per file), and the segments are grafted back at the right offset. The full decode is untouched, so the other windows cannot move — moving them is what sank the per-region decode (results/2026-10-15-vad.md).

**References** (2 files, 0.76 h): it-002 (it, 17 min, adjudicated), en-002 (en, 29 min, adjudicated).

## Systems

- `turbo-vad`: large-v3-turbo, language detected, only stretches with sound (VAD), skipped sound regions decoded again on their own clip (gap repair)
- `turbo-vad-norepair`: large-v3-turbo, language detected, VAD without gap repair (comparison)

## The real case

Mic track of `call-20261023-113020` (9 min, Italian), the recording that exposed the bug:

| | Without repair | With repair |
|---|---|---|
| Words in 180.05–206.38 s | **0** | **60** (correct Italian, checked by reading) |
| Uncovered regions ≥ 3 s left | 2 (180.05–206.38, 211.76–237.07) | 0 |
| Decode time | 34.5 s | 47.1 s |

Both skipped windows are recovered; the +12.6 s pays for re-decoding ~52 s of clips.

## Decision

Adopted, on by default in the `vad=True` path (`gap_repair=True`): the WER does not move, the test-set cost is +10% on one file and zero on the other, and the real case is fully recovered. `turbo-vad-norepair` stays in the systems list for future comparisons.

## How to read the numbers

- **Fidelity WER** is the primary metric (decision 0010): it counts whether the words are the ones spoken, without rewording; fillers, repetitions, truncated words and false starts do not count. **subst.** = substitutions as a % of the words: the direct sign of rewording.
- **Literal WER**: only fillers are removed; repetitions and truncated words count.
- **RTF**: processing time / audio duration, on the Mac (M4 Pro).
- **Reproducibility**: fixed MLX random seed (0). Whisper's temperature fallback samples at random: without a seed, two runs of the same system give different numbers.
- **Silver references**: words checked by a person, fillers mostly proposed by Whisper large-v3-turbo. The filler metrics are optimistic for the turbo variants, especially with the prompt.
- **Anchoring**: the references start from the large-v3-turbo draft, so the turbo systems have an advantage over the other models.
- With few references the intervals are wide: differences within the intervals are not significant.
